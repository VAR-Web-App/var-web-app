# KeystonePro — What's New

A plain-language running list of the major additions and improvements, newest
at the top. Built for Barry — no jargon, just what's new and what it does for
you on a real project.

_Last updated: 2026-08-03._

---

## 🧾 Read a supplier invoice from an email — _newest_

When a vendor emails you a PDF invoice or material list, open the attachment
right in the app (the **View** button on the email) and hit **Parse**. It now
tells you plainly what happened instead of leaving you guessing:

- **A clear result.** "Read 14 line items · $6,849.52 from this document,"
  plus the invoice number, date, and vendor it pulled out.
- **You know where it went.** The attachment is filed on that project's
  **Files** — the note says so.
- **One tap to track the cost.** **Save as invoice** drops the line items and
  total onto the project and links you straight to the **Finances** tab to see
  it against your budget — no re-typing.

## 📥 Email that files itself — _still polishing_

Your client email now lands on the right project on its own.

- **One-click connect.** Link your Gmail or Outlook in a few seconds (read-only
  — the app can *read* to file your mail, it never sends on your behalf).
- **Auto-files to the project.** When a client emails you, it shows up on that
  project's **Correspondence** log — matched by the client's email address or
  the project name. Junk (receipts, notifications, newsletters) stays out.
- **"Needs reply" list.** An unanswered client email becomes a to-do in your
  **Inbox**, and the project gets a **blue dot**, until you handle it. **Reply**
  opens the message in Gmail; **Mark done** clears it.
- **Gets smarter as you use it.** Replies in an ongoing conversation file
  automatically, and once you assign an email from someone new (a spouse,
  architect, or lender) to a project, their future email files itself too.
- **Nothing lost.** Mail it can't confidently place lands in an **Unassigned**
  review list to assign in one tap (or dismiss) — never silently dropped.

## 📄 Paper-trail export

One button on any project prints a complete **PDF record** — every request,
change order, sign-off, and email, in order. Made for the "that's not what I
asked for" conversation eight months in.

## ✅ Turn a client email into a tracked request

One tap on a client's email logs it as a **Request** on the project — their
exact words, timestamped — which you can tie to a phase and the sub who'll do
it, and convert to a signed change order.

## 📝 Requests log

- Capture what a client asks for, **in their words**, tied to the **phase and
  sub** who'll execute it — so it resurfaces months later when that sub shows
  up (the "fireplace nuance during framing" problem).
- **One tap turns a request into a change order** (with the numbers and client
  sign-off).

## 🔔 Notifications by project

Every project has an **activity feed** — milestones, change orders, payments,
and requests as they happen, with a "new" badge.

## 💰 Finances, consolidated

- A **"Needs attention"** strip at the top surfaces cash shortfalls, subs over
  budget, and thin margins **first** — before you go digging.
- Panels grouped into **Forecast / Ledger / Sourcing**, and long tables collapse
  so the tab isn't a scroll wall.

## 📅 Scheduling

- A real **5-day weather forecast** per project (icon + high/low + rain %), not
  just a bad-weather warning.
- **Conflict alerts only when there's an actual double-booking** — no empty
  "all clear" noise.

## ✨ Quality-of-life

- **Info tooltips** on every page header and the nav — hover to see what each
  screen does.
- **Editable client contact** (name + email) on each project.
- **Sample data covers every project stage**, and **"Reset to demo data"** gives
  you a clean slate anytime.
